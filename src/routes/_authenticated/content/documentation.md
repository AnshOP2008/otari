# TaskBase guide

## Upload and search sources

Open **KnowledgeBase** to upload a text PDF, Markdown file, or plain text file up to 8 MB. Search by a deadline, phrase, or project name to preview passages the assistant can use. Search and upload run without AI credits. Scanned PDFs need a text layer.

## Chat with evidence

Open **Chat** and choose all sources or one source. Clear **Search knowledge base** to ask about your board without retrieving new excerpts. Expand **Sources cited** below a reply to see its numbered passages. Saved replies retain the excerpts they cite.

## Build a plan

Open **Planning Desk** and paste a handoff or load a saved source into the brief. Check what will be sent to Otari and enter your available hours. The planner drafts tasks and a second model reviews them. Choose which tasks to add to your board. **Otari run details** shows the models and request IDs for live runs.

You can also select **Find New Tasks** on the board to append supported tasks from your sources and recent chat. Existing board items remain available.

## Connect Google

In **Integrations**, grant Calendar or Gmail read access for the import you need. Select a calendar and date range for events. For Gmail, choose an inbox count, unread filter, or a search expression. Preview the matches, inspect the captured text, and select the entries you want to save. Confirming replaces that source's saved snapshot in your knowledge base.

To send a task to Google Calendar, choose one open task with a due date and use **Send selected task** after enabling Calendar write. You can also **Prepare calendar file** and download the .ics without a Google connection.
